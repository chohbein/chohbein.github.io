Histopathologic Cancer Detection PyTorch · CNNs · GPU Training · scikit-learn
  • Compared two CNN architectures to predict the presence of tumors in histopathologic images, as a final project for a deep learning course.
  • Tuned for a laptop GPU that was overheating on long training runs — mixed precision, multiple workers, image downsizing, and batch-size tuning.
  • H0: fine-grained detail wins — small kernels, minimal pooling, padding to keep edge information, light dropout.
  • Ha: broad trends win — larger kernels, aggressive pooling, heavy dropout.
  • Final improvements: early stopping, LR scheduling, image augmentation.
  • Result: the fine-detail model scored better — ROC-AUC 0.981, with the false-negative rate prioritized down to 2%.

Monet Painting GAN PyTorch · CycleGAN · CLIP Embeddings · Style Transfer
  • Unpaired image style transfer to turn photos into Monet-style paintings.
  • CycleGAN baseline, stabilized with spectral normalization, label smoothing, noise injection, delayed discriminator updates, mixed precision, and LR tuning.
  • CLIP-conditioned GAN: frozen CLIP embeddings guide the generator semantically, a U-Net generator preserves spatial detail, and CLIP perceptual loss preserves content while applying style.
  • Result: the CLIP-conditioned approach produced higher-quality generations than the baseline in one third of the training time.
